# Post-PSC higher-dimensional substitution-tiling research program

**Status:** candidate research program. This document does not register a new theorem.

## Core question

Find a mathematically natural class \(\mathcal C\) of substitution tilings in dimension \(d\ge 2\) for which finite exact obstruction theory forces coincidence uniformly:

\[
T\in\mathcal C
\Longrightarrow
\text{overlap/algebraic coincidence}
\Longrightarrow
\text{pure discrete dynamical spectrum}.
\]

The spectral implication is intended to be an audited imported result under exact hypotheses. The new mathematical burden is the class-wide coincidence implication.

## Transferable PSC architecture

The transferable idea is not one-dimensional interval order. It is the proof pattern:

1. encode exact local disagreement states;
2. close them under inflation/substitution;
3. reduce failure to a recurrent noncoincident component;
4. retain enough ordered/geometric information that growth alone cannot erase;
5. force coincidence or contradict class hypotheses;
6. keep the spectral theorem as a separately audited import.

Every step whose PSC proof used scalar displacement, left/right order, or balanced-pair structure must be re-established in the higher-dimensional setting.

## Research tracks

### A. Select the theorem class

Compare candidate classes with hypotheses separated into substitution dynamics, expansion algebra, FLC, Meyer structure, tile topology, representability, and coincidence-criterion prerequisites.

### B. Define the exact overlap object

Generalize \((i,j,t)\) to \((i,j,z)\) with vector/algebraic displacement and inflation

\[
z'=Qz+q-p.
\]

Exact representation and exact intersection are theorem-facing choices.

### C. Recurrent obstruction normal form

Once completeness/finiteness is established, failure should reduce to a recurrent noncoincident component carrying exact translations, ordered children, multiplicities, expansion action, and any required context.

### D. Automatic coincidence

Seek a class-wide contradiction using mechanisms such as Meyer uniform discreteness, internal-space recurrence, conjugate contraction, boundary/contact restrictions, recognizability, homology/cohomology, or maximal-equicontinuous-factor structure.

### E. Spectral import

Only after coincidence is established, invoke a pinned source with every hypothesis mapped to a repository result/input and distinguish dynamical pure point spectrum, diffraction, and topological statements.

## Non-goals

- all substitution tilings;
- Penrose as the theorem statement;
- assuming regular model-set status and calling its known spectral consequence new;
- finite censuses as class theorems;
- floating-point intersection as exact geometry;
- hidden unimodularity or connectedness.
