## Repository status vocabulary

Until the estate contract is deliberately changed, use only the registered classes:

- **candidate** — research direction captured, not established;
- **open** — precise unresolved obligation;
- **finite-domain** — committed exhaustive finite certificate;
- **conditional** — valid only under stated hypotheses or a pending dependency;
- **imported** — external result used with source/hypotheses recorded;
- **retired** — abandoned, superseded, or falsified.

Planning terminology such as “evidence,” “bridge,” or “proof target” may describe roles in prose but is not a claim status.

## Counterexample discipline

A failed proposed lemma should retain:

- substitution/tile data;
- expansion matrix;
- exact overlap state(s);
- recurrence/cycle witness;
- hypothesis checklist;
- the asserted implication that failed.
